I am a Computer Science MS student and Graduate Research Assistant at Texas State University.

Originally from Bangladesh, I earned my BS in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Dhaka. During my undergraduate studies, I worked as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Green Networking Research (GNR) Group under the supervision of Dr. Md. Abdur Razzaque. This work led to a first-author paper published in Ad Hoc Networks in July 2024. The paper combines mean-shift clustering and multi-agent deep reinforcement learning for collision-aware path planning in coalitions of unmanned aerial and ground vehicles operating in obstacled environments.

After graduation, I worked as a data engineer at Enosis Solutions, a software development company in Bangladesh, and as a lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Shanto-Mariam University of Creative Technology.
