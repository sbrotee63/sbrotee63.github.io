I am currently a Computer Science MS student at Texas State University. I am employed as a Graduate Research Assistant here, working under the supervision of Dr. Chul-Ho Lee. I have been working on the applications of diffusion models and flow matching in molecular graph generation.

Originally from Bangladesh, I earned my BS degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Dhaka. I worked as a data engineer in Enosis Solutions, a software development company in Bangladesh, right after my graduation. My next adventure was as a faculty lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Shanto-Mariam University of Creative Technology, Bangladesh. I have also worked as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Green Networking Research (GNR) Group at the University of Dhaka under the supervision of Dr. Md. Abdur Razzaque. While there, I published a paper as the first author in Ad Hoc Networks, Elsevier (A Q1 journal) in July 2024. It involves designing a minimal-collision, optimal path toward specific destinations with coalitions of UAV-UGV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles - Unmanned Ground Vehicles) in an obstructed environment. The work involved creating a hybrid framework integrating mean-shift clustering and multi-agent deep reinforcement learning.

My research interests lie in diffusion models, flow matching, molecular graph generations, multi-agent deep reinforcement learning, and applying ML strategies in cyber-physical systems. My current objective is to become a proficient CS researcher and make meaningful contributions to my research fields during my graduate studies.

I am an inquisitive person who is always willing to learn more and explore new ideas. I possess a strong ability to perform well under pressure within time constraints and consistently deliver results within tight deadlines.
